# IAM Findings Report

## Overview
An Identity and Access Management (IAM) review was conducted to evaluate user account permissions and identify potential security risks.

---

# Findings

## Excessive Privileges
One employee account was identified with administrator access beyond business necessity.

### Risk
Excessive privileges increase the risk of unauthorized changes, insider threats, and account compromise.

### Recommendation
Apply the Principle of Least Privilege by reducing permissions to only required access levels.

---

## Dormant Account
A former employee account remained active after separation.

### Risk
Dormant accounts may be exploited by unauthorized individuals.

### Recommendation
Implement account deprovisioning procedures immediately upon employee termination.

---

## Shared Credentials
Shared account usage was identified within the sales department.

### Risk
Shared credentials reduce accountability and increase security risks.

### Recommendation
Require unique individual user accounts for all employees.
