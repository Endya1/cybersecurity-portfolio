# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to ensure proper and secure use of company technology resources.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party users.

## Policy
Users must:
- Use company systems for authorized business purposes only
- Protect sensitive information
- Avoid downloading unauthorized software
- Report suspicious activity immediately

Users must not:
- Share passwords
- Access unauthorized systems
- Disable security controls
- Use company devices for illegal activity

## Enforcement
Violations may result in disciplinary action.
