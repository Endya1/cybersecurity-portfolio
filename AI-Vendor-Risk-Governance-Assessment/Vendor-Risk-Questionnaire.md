# AI Vendor Risk Questionnaire

## Vendor Governance Assessment

### Governance & Oversight
1. Does the organization maintain formal AI governance policies?
2. Is there a documented Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) review process?
3. Are AI system decisions reviewable and explainable?

### Security Controls
4. Is multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for administrative access?
5. Are audit logs maintained for AI system activity?
6. Does the vendor conduct regular security assessments?

### Privacy & Compliance
7. Is training data sourced from HIPAA-compliant datasets?
8. Are patient records encrypted during storage and transmission?
9. Are privacy impact assessments conducted regularly?

### Bias & Fairness
10. How does the vendor monitor for algorithmic bias?
11. Are fairness evaluations conducted across demographic groups?
12. How are hallucinations identified and mitigated?

### Incident Response
13. Does the vendor maintain an AI-specific incident response plan?
14. Are security incidents reported within defined timelines?

### Risk Management
15. Are third-party vendor reassessments conducted regularly?
16. Is there a documented AI risk management process?
