# Bias Impact Assessment

## Objective
This assessment evaluates potential algorithmic bias risks associated with the HealthAI Diagnostics system.

---

# Simulated Fairness Test

## Scenario
The AI system was evaluated to determine whether diagnostic recommendations differed across demographic groups.

---

# Simulated Results

| Group | Approval / Positive Diagnostic Rate |
|---|---|
| Group A | 82% |
| Group B | 68% |

---

# Observations

- Group B received significantly lower positive diagnostic outcomes.
- Potential bias indicators suggest unequal model performance across demographic groups.
- Additional fairness testing and model validation are recommended.

---

# Potential Risks

- Unequal patient treatment outcomes
- Regulatory and compliance concerns
- Reputational damage
- Increased legal and ethical risk

---

# Recommendations

- Conduct ongoing fairness testing
- Improve dataset diversity
- Implement human review procedures
- Perform quarterly bias audits
- Increase model transparency and explainability

---

# Overall Bias Risk Rating

## MEDIUM TO HIGH

The observed variance between demographic groups indicates potential fairness concerns requiring additional governance oversight and testing.
