# AI Risk Assessment

## AI System Description
A hypothetical AI system is used to automate customer recommendations and basic decision-making processes.

---

## Identified Risks

### 1. Bias in AI Decision-Making
- Risk: AI may produce unfair or biased outcomes based on training data.
- Impact: High
- Likelihood: Medium
- Mitigation:
  - Regular model testing for bias
  - Use diverse training datasets
  - Human oversight in decisions

---

### 2. Privacy Risks
- Risk: AI system may process sensitive user data without proper safeguards.
- Impact: High
- Likelihood: Medium
- Mitigation:
  - Data minimization practices
  - Encryption of sensitive data
  - Compliance with privacy standards (GDPR/CCPA concepts)

---

### 3. Model Manipulation / Adversarial Attacks
- Risk: AI model could be manipulated through malicious inputs.
- Impact: High
- Likelihood: Low
- Mitigation:
  - Input validation
  - Model monitoring
  - Security testing

---

### 4. Lack of Transparency
- Risk: AI decisions may not be explainable to users or auditors.
- Impact: Medium
- Likelihood: High
- Mitigation:
  - Use explainable AI methods
  - Document decision logic
  - Provide audit logs
