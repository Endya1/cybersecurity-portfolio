# AI Risk Register

| Risk | Category | Impact | Likelihood | Risk Level | Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bias in AI outputs | Ethical | High | Medium | High | Bias testing + diverse datasets |
| Privacy leakage | Security | High | Medium | High | Encryption + data minimization |
| Model misuse | Security | High | Low | Medium | Access controls + monitoring |
| Lack of transparency | Governance | Medium | High | High | Explainable AI + audit logs |
