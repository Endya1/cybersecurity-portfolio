# AI Governance Controls

## Purpose
To define governance controls for responsible and secure use of AI systems.

---

## Governance Controls

### 1. Human Oversight
All critical AI decisions must include human review before final execution.

### 2. Data Governance
All AI training data must be reviewed for:
- accuracy
- bias
- privacy compliance

### 3. Model Monitoring
AI systems must be continuously monitored for:
- performance degradation
- abnormal behavior
- unexpected outputs

### 4. Access Control
Only authorized personnel may:
- train AI models
- modify AI systems
- deploy updates

### 5. Documentation
All AI systems must maintain:
- model versioning
- risk logs
- decision audit trails
