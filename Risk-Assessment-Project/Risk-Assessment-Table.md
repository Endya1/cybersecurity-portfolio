# Risk Assessment Table

| Asset | Threat | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level | Recommended Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Employee Accounts | Weak Passwords | High | High | High | Enforce MFA and strong password policy |
| Company Laptop | Malware Infection | Medium | High | High | Install endpoint protection software |
| Email System | Phishing Attacks | High | High | High | Security awareness training |
| Cloud Storage | Unauthorized Access | Medium | High | Medium | Role-based access controls |
| Business Network | Ransomware | Medium | Critical | High | Regular backups and network monitoring |
