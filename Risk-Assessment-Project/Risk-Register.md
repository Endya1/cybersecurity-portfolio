# Risk Register

## Risk 1: Weak Passwords
- Risk Owner: IT Department
- Risk Level: High
- Mitigation Strategy:
  - Enforce strong password requirements
  - Require MFA
  - Conduct password security training

---

## Risk 2: Phishing Attacks
- Risk Owner: Security Team
- Risk Level: High
- Mitigation Strategy:
  - Employee phishing awareness training
  - Email filtering protections
  - Incident reporting procedures

---

## Risk 3: Ransomware
- Risk Owner: System Administrator
- Risk Level: High
- Mitigation Strategy:
  - Maintain secure backups
  - Deploy endpoint protection
  - Monitor suspicious activity
